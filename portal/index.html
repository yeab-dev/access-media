Make your lessons work for every learner

You make lesson videos. Put them on your website with our player and every lesson gets English and Amharic captions, an Ethiopian Sign Language window, audio description for blind learners, a transcript, and a small download for slow phones. Free and open source.

Get your key Read the documentation

Three steps

  1. Get your key

    Type your organisation's name. You get a key at once, plus a practice lesson to try everything on.

    Get your key

  2. Add a lesson

    Upload a video. We make it small enough for 3G phones and write captions from the speech in it.

    Add a lesson

  3. Put it on your page

    Paste two lines where the lesson should play. That is all your website needs.

    <script type="module" src="https://this-server/module/db-player.js"></script>
<db-player lesson="your-lesson"></db-player>

Everything you can do

Each one works in the browser (the links) and through the API (the documentation has an example for every call).

  • Lessons

    Upload videos, change titles (also in Amharic), delete lessons, see all of yours with the code for each page.

    Add a lesson

  • Captions

    Automatic first draft from the speech; you correct the words and times. English, Amharic and any other language.

    Caption editor

  • Audio description

    Short lines that say what is on screen. The player reads them aloud for blind learners.

    Write descriptions

  • Ethiopian Sign Language

    An interpreter records in the browser while the lesson plays. Learners can move and resize the window.

    Sign language studio

  • Documents and offline

    Each lesson also becomes a web page, a tagged PDF and an offline pack for slow phones. Add slide descriptions for the pictures.

    How

  • Jobs

    Job cards that read themselves aloud, in English and Amharic, and that job search engines understand.

    How

  • Certificates

    A link learners send to employers. The employer's page checks it is real.

    See an example

  • Webhooks

    Your own system hears when a lesson is ready, captions change or a sign video arrives.

    How

Your learners' privacy

No accounts for learners and no tracking. Lesson audio goes only to the speech model on this server, never to an outside service. For a key we keep only the name you type.
