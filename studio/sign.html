Sign language studio

For Ethiopian Sign Language interpreters. Record yourself signing a lesson. The recording is lined up with the lesson automatically and appears in the lesson's sign language window.

1. Choose the lesson

Ask the person who runs the server. It is only needed to save.

2. Get ready

  1. Plain background. A top that contrasts with your skin. Light on your face and hands.
  2. Your upper body, both hands and your face must be in the picture.
  3. Wear headphones if you can, so the lesson sound is not recorded. (Sound is removed anyway.)

3. Record

After a 3-second countdown the lesson starts from the beginning and recording starts with it. Sign along. Recording stops when the lesson ends, or when you press Stop.

4. Check and save

Play both together. If your signing is a little early or late, move the slider until it matches.

Right: your signing starts later in the recording, so more is cut from its start. Left: the opposite.
