How to write a CV

Lesson notes

A CV is a short document that tells an employer who you are and what you can do. Employers read many CVs, so keep yours clear and short.

What to put in your CV

  1. Your name, phone number and town at the top, so the employer can call you.
  2. Education and training: schools, courses and certificates, newest first.
  3. Work experience: jobs, volunteering and family business work. Say what you did in each one.

Keep it short

One or two pages is enough. Ask a friend to read it and check the spelling before you send it.
