About Debo Learning

“Debo” is the Ethiopian tradition of neighbours working together on a big job. We help young people get ready for work the same way: together, one small step at a time.

What we do

We make short video lessons on the skills employers ask for, and we connect learners who finish a course with employers who are hiring.

Who our lessons are for

Young people in Ethiopia who are looking for their first job. Most of our learners watch on a phone.
